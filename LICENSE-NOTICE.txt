AirPlay Pro
===========

AirPlay Pro is a Windows tray app that runs the open-source UxPlay AirPlay
receiver (https://github.com/FDH2/UxPlay).

UxPlay is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Because AirPlay Pro
ships UxPlay, AirPlay Pro as a whole is also distributed under the GNU GPL v3.0.
The full UxPlay license is in uxplay\licenses\UxPlay-LICENSE.txt after
installation. The UxPlay version that was built is recorded in
uxplay\licenses\UxPlay-VERSION.txt, and its source is available from the link
above.

The bundled GStreamer libraries and plugins (https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org)
are licensed under the LGPL, with some plugins under other open-source licenses.
